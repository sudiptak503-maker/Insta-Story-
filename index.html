Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

Public Privacy Policy URL: Before publishing, host this policy at a publicly accessible HTTPS URL and use that exact URL in Play Console. The in-app text alone does not provide the required public URL.

Golpo ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account info: Name, email address
  • Profile: Bio, profile photo
  • Content: Posts, stories, reels, comments, messages you share
  • Usage: Device info, app activity for security

2. How We Use Your Info

  • To provide and improve our services
  • To connect you with other users
  • To keep the platform safe
  • To respond to your support requests

3. Data Sharing

We do NOT sell your personal data. We only share with trusted service providers (Cloudinary for media hosting, Firebase for database & auth) that follow strict data protection standards.

4. Your Rights

  • Access, edit, or delete your data anytime
  • Request account deletion from Profile → Menu → Delete Account. Some records may require server-side cleanup.
  • Contact us at support@golpo.app

Data Retention and Deletion

Account deletion is intended to remove account data, but records in chat subcollections, third-party media hosting (Cloudinary), backups, and legally required moderation records may not be removed by this client-side code. The app owner must implement and test secure server-side cleanup and disclose any retention periods before launch.

5. Data Security

We rely on Firebase and configured service providers for data handling. Security depends on correctly configured Firebase Security Rules, provider settings, and transport security. No internet transmission is 100% secure.

6. Children's Privacy

Golpo is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us immediately.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. Continued use means you accept the changes.

8. Contact

Email: support@golpo.app
